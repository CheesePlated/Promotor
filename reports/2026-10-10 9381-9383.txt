ID 9382
Emoji Auctions (AI=1.0)
class: ordinary
author: Ronic
coauthors: 


Enact a new Rule of Power 1 entitled "Emoji Auctions" with the following text:

{

An Emoji is an untracked, fixed, indestructible asset with Power 0.
Exactly one of each of the following Emojis exists:

* Rock Emoji: 🪨 (U+1FAA8)
* Top Hat Emoji: 🎩 (U+1F3A9)
* Sun Emoji: ☀️ (U+2600 U+FE0F)
* Ear of Rice Emoji: 🌾 (U+1F33E)
* Gem Stone Emoji: 💎 (U+1F48E)
* Black Joker Emoji: 🃏 (U+1F0CF)
* Scales Emoji: ⚖️ (U+2696 U+FE0F)
* Scroll Emoji: 📜 (U+1F4DC)
* Ribbon Emoji: 🎀 (U+1F380)
* Ballot Box Emoji: 🗳️ (U+1F5F3 U+FE0F)
* Trophy Emoji: 🏆 (U+1F3C6)
* Cyclone Emoji: 🌀 (U+1F300)
* Red Heart Emoji: ❤️ (U+2764 U+FE0F)
* Fire Emoji: 🔥 (U+1F525)
* Seedling Emoji: 🌱 (U+1F331)
* Crown Emoji: 👑 (U+1F451)

Each Emoji is initially owned by Agora. Emojis can be owned only by
Agora and current or former players. If an Emoji would otherwise be in
abeyance, it is transferred to Agora. These transfers are explicitly
permitted despite Emojis being fixed.

Ownership of an Emoji has no effect under this Rule other than
identifying its owner.

An Emoji is available if Agora owns it.

An Emoji auction is ongoing from its initiation until it closes. No
Emoji auction CAN be initiated while another is ongoing, and no more
than one Emoji auction CAN be initiated in an Agoran month.

A player CAN initiate an Emoji auction by announcement in
Agora-Business, identifying an available Emoji and an integer N of at
least 100, and paying a fee of N+10 Pyrite. The identified Emoji
becomes the auctioned Emoji, that player becomes the leader, and N
becomes the leading bid.

An Emoji auction closes exactly seven days after its initiation.

While an Emoji auction is ongoing, a player other than the leader CAN
replace a leading bid of M Pyrite with a bid of N Pyrite by
announcement in Agora-Business and paying a fee of N Pyrite, provided
N is an integer at least 100 greater than M. Upon doing so, and only
once for that action, the former leader is granted M Pyrite if e is
then a player. The bidder becomes the leader and N becomes the leading
bid.

The leader CAN raise a leading bid from M Pyrite to N Pyrite by
announcement in Agora-Business and paying a fee of N-M Pyrite,
provided N is an integer at least 100 greater than M. No Pyrite is
granted as a result of such a self-raise.

A leading bid CANNOT be withdrawn or reduced.

A bid is effective only if date-stamped before the auction's closing
time. If two auction actions have the same date-stamped time, they are
processed in the order in which they appear in the public
Agora-Business archive. If that archive does not determine an order,
the message with the lexicographically earlier Message-ID is processed
first. Player identity is not used to determine priority.

When an Emoji auction closes, the auctioned Emoji is transferred from
Agora to the leader. This transfer is explicitly permitted despite the
Emoji being fixed.

}

[Non-operative rationale:

Agora has many numbers and not enough tiny objects of irrational desire.

The initial collection contains sixteen recognizable symbols drawn
mostly from Agoran culture. One prepaid auction per month focuses
attention on one object. The opening fee discourages wasting the
monthly slot, and the 100-Pyrite minimum increment discourages bid
spam.

Every leading bid is fully prepaid. When someone is outbid, e receives
an equal refund. The eventual winning bid remains destroyed, so there
is no escrow, debt, default, officer workload, or later collection
step.

Ownership is intentionally cosmetic. Future proposals can add Emojis,
enable other methods of circulation, or carefully add effects if Agora
wants to extend the system.]

ID 9383
Color (AI=1.0)
class: ordinary
author: Galle
coauthors: ais523, Murphy


Enact a new power 1 rule, entitled "Color", with the following text:
---
Color is a secured player switch with values grey (default), green, purple, and orange. The Paintor is an office, responsible for tracking color. Whenever an active player is grey, the Paintor SHALL, in a timely fashion, randomly select either green, purple, or orange, and announce the player and the result. Upon such an announcement, the player's color is switched to the selected value.

For each non-default color, the first time per week that a proposal submitted by a player of that color is adopted, all other players of that color gain 5 radiance.

A player CAN, but SHALL NOT, flip eir color to green, purple, or orange once per month by announcement.

Players are ENCOURAGED to attribute great significance to eir color and what it says about em as a person.
---

[This is mostly a silly subgame intended to provide a simple alternative way to gain radiance, so that we could potentially make radiance wins require a higher point total. There is at least one intentional ambiguity here but hopefully nothing catastrophically broken.]
